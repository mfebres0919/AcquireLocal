Who we are

AcquireLocal is a community initiative, founded by Dana K. Michel and Lori Ann Pope, that connects aspiring entrepreneurs and legacy business owners with education, practical resources, and specialists in business acquisition and succession. This policy explains what information we collect through this website and how we use it.

Information we collect

We only collect information you choose to give us:

  • Community sign-up. When you join the AcquireLocal community, we collect your first name, last name (optional), email address, and the topics you are interested in (for example, acquiring a business, exploring succession, sharing expertise, or partnerships).
  • Email updates. When you use the email sign-up bar, we collect your email address.

We do not use advertising trackers or analytics cookies on this website.

How we use your information

We use the information you provide to:

  • Send you updates about upcoming AcquireLocal events and ways to get involved.
  • Follow up on partnership inquiries.
  • Understand which topics matter most to our community, so we can plan useful programming.

We do not sell or rent your personal information.

Service providers

We use a small number of trusted services to run this website. They process information only to provide their service to us:

  • Eventbrite handles event registration, ticket checkout, and payment. Anything you enter during checkout is collected by Eventbrite under its own policy, and Eventbrite may use cookies. Eventbrite privacy policy (opens in a new tab)
  • Netlify hosts this website and receives our sign-up form submissions, which it stores and forwards to us by email. Netlify may also keep standard server logs (such as IP address and browser type) for security and reliability. Netlify privacy policy (opens in a new tab)

Links to other websites, such as Google Maps, are governed by those websites’ own privacy policies.

How long we keep it

We keep your information for as long as you remain part of the AcquireLocal community or until you ask us to remove it.

Your choices

  • Unsubscribe at any time using the link in any email we send, or by contacting us.
  • Access, correct, or delete your information by contacting us. We will respond within 30 days.

Children

This website is intended for adults. We do not knowingly collect information from children under 13.

Changes to this policy

We may update this policy from time to time. When we do, we will change the “Last updated” date at the top of this page.
