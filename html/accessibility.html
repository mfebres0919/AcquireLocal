Accessibility Statement

AcquireLocal wants everyone to be able to learn, connect, and take part. Here is how we make this website accessible, and how to reach us if something gets in your way.

Our commitment

AcquireLocal is committed to making our website usable by as many people as possible, including people with disabilities. We are building a community that welcomes aspiring entrepreneurs, legacy business owners, and specialists, and that welcome extends to how people experience our website.

Standard we follow

We aim to meet the Web Content Accessibility Guidelines (WCAG) 2.1, Level AA, published by the World Wide Web Consortium (W3C). These guidelines explain how to make web content more accessible for people with a wide range of disabilities, including visual, auditory, physical, speech, cognitive, and neurological disabilities.

What we have done

Accessibility was considered from the start of this website’s design and development. Measures include:

  • Readable contrast. Text meets or exceeds the WCAG AA contrast ratio of 4.5:1, including text placed over photographs.
  • Keyboard access. Every link, button, menu, and form field can be reached and used with a keyboard, with a clearly visible focus outline. A “Skip to main content” link appears as the first item on every page.
  • Screen reader support. Pages use a logical heading structure, descriptive link text, labelled form fields, and text alternatives for meaningful images. Decorative images are hidden from assistive technology.
  • Control over motion. The rotating photos on our home page can be paused at any time. If your device is set to reduce motion, animations and the slideshow are turned off automatically.
  • Flexible layouts. Pages adapt to screens as narrow as 320 pixels and to browser zoom up to 400% without loss of content.
  • Clear forms. Required fields are marked, and confirmation and error messages are announced to assistive technology.

We test with automated tools (including axe-core and Google Lighthouse) and by hand, using a keyboard and screen readers.

Third-party content

Some features rely on services we do not control. We choose providers with accessibility in mind, but we cannot guarantee their content fully meets WCAG 2.1 AA:

  • Eventbrite provides event registration and ticket checkout.
  • Google Maps provides directions to the event venue.

If you have trouble registering, contact us and we will help you reserve your seat another way.

Compatibility

This website is designed to work with current versions of major browsers, including Chrome, Safari, Firefox, and Edge, on desktop and mobile, and with common assistive technologies such as VoiceOver, NVDA, JAWS, and TalkBack.

Feedback and help

We welcome your feedback. If you experience any difficulty using this website, or would like information in a different format, please let us know and we will do our best to help.
