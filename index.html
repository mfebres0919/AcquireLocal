About AcquireLocal

One generation is asking what’s next. Another is asking who will carry it forward.

Across the country, experienced professionals are considering business ownership as a way to put their skills and experience to work. At the same time, longtime business owners are thinking about succession and what will happen to the businesses they have spent years building.

Two Roads

Two roads to ownership

For retiring small business owners, a prepared buyer or an employee-ownership transition can protect jobs and preserve the business’s role in the community. AcquireLocal introduces two succession strategies.

Road 01 Our primary focus

Entrepreneurship through Acquisition (ETA)

Buying and growing an existing business, so the new owner can focus on scaling rather than building.

What the new owner inherits

  • Trained employees
  • Supplier relationships
  • Brand recognition
  • Operational systems
Road 02 35+ employees

Employee Ownership (EO)

A powerful tool for increasing employee engagement and stronger business outcomes. It is a consideration for small businesses with 35 or more employees.

AcquireLocal focuses primarily on ETA for smaller businesses as a road to ownership. Not sure which road fits? Join the community →
