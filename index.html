About AcquireLocal

One generation is asking what’s next. Another is asking who will carry it forward.

Across the country, experienced professionals are considering business ownership as a way to put their skills and experience to work. At the same time, longtime business owners are thinking about succession and what will happen to the businesses they have spent years building.

Acquisition focus Up to $10M

AcquireLocal focuses on acquisitions of businesses up to $10 million.

Deals in this range often combine financing sources, such as SBA-backed and CDFI loans, bank debt, owner financing, and buyer equity. CDFIs and mission lenders can participate through a Community Lending Partner role supporting financing needs.

The Event

Our First Event: Brooklyn

We are bringing these two groups together on November 12 for the inaugural AcquireLocal event in Brooklyn.

Business owners, aspiring entrepreneurs, lenders, advisors, and community organizations will share real experiences and practical information about acquiring or transitioning an established business.

It is also an opportunity to meet people who can help move these conversations forward.

Why It Matters

Nobody wins when these businesses close.

There is an opportunity to preserve those businesses and the jobs, relationships, and services they provide, while opening a path to ownership for the next generation of entrepreneurs.

An estimated 17,600 Brooklyn business owners are expected to retire within 10 years, and many haven’t decided what comes next.

Estimate applies the 38% of NYC owners expecting retirement within 10 years to Brooklyn’s 46,300 small businesses; it does not forecast completed sales. Sources: NYCEDC (2024); Chase Local Snapshot succession survey (2026).
